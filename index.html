Our Mission

We believe every Persian-American child deserves to hold their language close — to read their grandparents' letters, to understand the poetry of Hafez, and to feel at home in both worlds.

How We Teach

Classes are small and welcoming, with a warm homeschool spirit. Each session blends speaking, listening, reading, and writing — all within the context of Persian culture and storytelling. No rote drills. Real language, real connection.

Who It's For

Children ages 5–14, at any level. Whether your child has never heard Farsi spoken, or already speaks it at home but can't yet read it — there is a place for them here.

برنامه درسی · Curriculum

Three Levels, One Journey

Each level builds gently on the last, keeping children engaged and moving forward at the right pace.

نور کوچک
Little Light
Beginner · Ages 5–7
  • Persian alphabet — letters and sounds
  • Numbers, colors, animals, and family words
  • Simple songs and nursery rhymes
  • Basic everyday phrases and greetings
  • Visual games and hands-on activities
نور رشد
Growing Light
Intermediate · Ages 8–11
  • Reading connected text and short stories
  • Writing sentences and short paragraphs
  • Vocabulary through Persian folktales
  • Everyday conversation practice
  • Introduction to Persian poetry
نور درخشان
Bright Light
Advanced · Ages 12–14
  • Reading literature and longer prose
  • Expressive and descriptive writing
  • Classical poetry — Hafez & Rumi
  • Persian cultural history and traditions
  • Independent reading projects
